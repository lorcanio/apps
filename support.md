# Support

Email **[support@lorcan.dev](mailto:support@lorcan.dev)** and we will reply as soon as we can. Please say which app you are using, your iPhone model and iOS version, and what happened.

Each app also has its own support page at `/<app>/support` with answers to common questions, such as restoring a purchase.

For refunds, use [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com): Apple handles them.
