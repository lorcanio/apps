# Schengen Calculator: UK Tax support

Email **[support@lorcan.dev](mailto:support@lorcan.dev)** and we will reply within five working days. Please say which iPhone and iOS version you use, and what you expected to see.

## Common questions

**How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?**
Sign in with the same Apple Account, open the app, then go to Settings and tap Restore Purchases.

**Do I need an account?**
No. The app has no account and no sign-in.

**Where are my trips kept?**
On your device, in your device backup, and in your own iCloud if iCloud sync is on. See the [privacy policy](/schengen/privacy).

**How do I move my trips to a new phone?**
Sign in to the same iCloud account with iCloud sync on. Your trips come back once the app opens. If you do not use iCloud, restore from a device backup.

**My count differs from what I expected. Why?**
The app counts only the trips you have entered, and counts both the arrival and the departure day for Schengen. A missing or wrong date changes the result. Open Summary, then "Show the working for this date" to see which days are counted.

**Does the app give tax or immigration advice?**
No. It is a record-keeping and counting tool. Border officials and HMRC make their own decisions.

**How do I get a refund?**
Refunds are handled by Apple. Use [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

**Can I share the app with my family?**
Pro is a single payment on your Apple Account. Family Sharing is off. Extra travellers can be added inside the app, each with their own counts.

## Not advice

Schengen Calculator helps you keep your own record of where you were. It is not legal, immigration or tax advice. The counts depend entirely on the dates you enter. Border officials and HMRC make their own decisions; check official sources or a qualified adviser before relying on these numbers.
