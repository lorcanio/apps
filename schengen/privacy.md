# Privacy policy: Schengen Calculator: UK Tax

<span class="meta">Last updated: 24 November 2026</span>

Schengen Calculator: UK Tax ("the app", called Schengen on your Home Screen) is made by lorcan.dev. This page explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## In short

The app has no account, no server of ours, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect your data. What you enter stays on your device and, if you leave iCloud sync on, in your own iCloud.

## What you enter

You enter trips by hand: dates, countries, and the names of any travellers you add. The app uses them to count your days.

This information is stored on your device. It is also included in your device backup, which Apple controls under its own privacy policy. We cannot see any of it. Deleting the app deletes it from your device.

## iCloud sync

iCloud sync is on by default and you can turn it off in Settings. When it is on, the app copies your trips and a few settings to your own iCloud, so they reach your other devices and come back on a new phone. Apple stores and protects this data under your Apple Account. We have no access to it and no server that receives it.

If you turn sync off, your trips stay on that device only.

## Notifications

If you turn on alerts, the app schedules notifications on your device before a Schengen or UK limit. They are created and shown on the device. They do not go through any service of ours.

## Exports

If you export your trips as a CSV or PDF, the file leaves the app only through the iOS share sheet, to the place you choose. We do not receive it.

## Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. We never receive your name, your Apple Account or your payment details. The app asks Apple, on your device, whether you have bought Pro. We see only Apple's combined sales reports, which do not identify you.

## Crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. If you have not, we receive nothing. You can change this in Settings, Privacy and Security, Analytics and Improvements.

## Location

The app does not use your location. You pick countries from a list.

## Contacting support

If you email us at [support@lorcan.dev](mailto:support@lorcan.dev), we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to reply to you. The app can open a draft in your mail app; nothing is sent unless you tap Send.

We keep support emails for 12 months after the conversation ends and then delete them. You can ask us to delete your emails sooner by writing to [support@lorcan.dev](mailto:support@lorcan.dev). We will do so within 30 days and confirm by reply.

## Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove your trips from the device. To remove the iCloud copy, turn off iCloud sync and manage the app's iCloud data in your device's Settings, under your name, iCloud. We hold nothing else about you apart from any support emails, which you can ask us to delete as above.

## Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

## Not advice

Schengen Calculator helps you keep your own record of where you were. It is not legal, immigration or tax advice. The counts depend entirely on the dates you enter. Border officials and HMRC make their own decisions; check official sources or a qualified adviser before relying on these numbers.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
