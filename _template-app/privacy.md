# Privacy policy: {{APP_NAME}}

Last updated: {{DATE}}

{{APP_NAME}} is made by {{DEVELOPER_NAME}}. This page explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

## In short

The app collects no personal data and has no account. Everything you enter stays on your device. There are no adverts, no analytics and no tracking.

## What stays on your device

The information you enter ({{WHAT_THE_USER_ENTERS}}) is stored only on your device. It is also included in your device backup, which Apple controls under its own privacy policy. We cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it from the device.

## Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. We never receive your name, your Apple Account or your payment details. The app asks Apple, on your device, whether you have bought {{APP_NAME}} Pro. We see only Apple's combined sales reports, which do not identify you.

## Crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. If you have not, we receive nothing. You can change this in Settings, Privacy and Security, Analytics and Improvements.

## Contacting support

If you email us at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to reply to you. The app opens a draft in your mail app with the app's version filled in; nothing is sent unless you tap Send.

We keep support emails for {{RETENTION_PERIOD, for example "12 months after the conversation ends"}} and then delete them. You can ask us to delete your emails sooner at any time by writing to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}; we will do so within {{DELETION_TIME, for example "30 days"}} and confirm by reply.

## Deleting your data

Data in the app is on your device only, so deleting the app deletes it. We hold nothing else about you apart from any support emails, which you can ask us to delete as above.

## Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.
