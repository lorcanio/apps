# Privacy

<span class="meta">Last updated: 19 November 2026</span>

This page covers this website, apps.lorcan.dev. Each app has its own privacy policy at `/<app>/privacy`, linked from the app's page and from its App Store listing.

## This website

The site sets no cookies, runs no scripts, and uses no analytics, adverts or trackers. It loads nothing from other websites. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub may log requests to it under [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement); we do not receive those logs.

## Email

If you write to [support@lorcan.dev](mailto:support@lorcan.dev), we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to reply to you.
