# ai-youtube keywords

A small command-line tool that reads keyword search metrics (search volume, competition and bid ranges) from Google Ads, so video topics can be planned around what people search for.

It runs on the computer of the person using it. You sign in with Google once and allow read access to your Google Ads data. The results are saved to files on that computer. Nothing is sent to lorcan.dev.
