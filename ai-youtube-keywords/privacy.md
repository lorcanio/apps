# Privacy policy: ai-youtube keywords

<span class="meta">Last updated: 20 November 2026</span>

ai-youtube keywords is made by lorcan.dev. This page explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## In short

The tool runs on your own computer. It reads keyword data from your Google Ads account and saves it to files on that computer. We do not receive it, store it, sell it or share it. There are no adverts, no analytics and no tracking.

## What the tool accesses

When you sign in with Google, you allow the tool to use the Google Ads scope (`https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords`). The tool uses that access only to:

- look up Keyword Planner metrics for keywords you give it: average monthly searches, competition and top-of-page bid ranges;
- read the performance of keywords in your own Google Ads accounts: impressions, clicks, cost and conversions.

It does not create, change or delete anything in your Google Ads account, and it does not read your email, files or any other Google service.

## Where your data goes

The data goes from Google to your computer and no further. Results are written to files in the folder you run the tool from. The sign-in credentials (a refresh token and the OAuth client details) are kept in a configuration file on your computer. There is no lorcan.dev server, database or account behind the tool, and we cannot see any of it.

## How we use Google user data

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including its Limited Use requirements. In particular, the data is used only to show you your keyword metrics. It is not transferred to anyone else, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI or machine learning models. No person at lorcan.dev reads it.

## Deleting your data and revoking access

The data is on your computer only, so deleting the result files and the configuration file deletes it. You can also withdraw the tool's access to your Google account at any time at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). After that, the saved sign-in credentials stop working.

## Contacting support

If you email us at [support@lorcan.dev](mailto:support@lorcan.dev), we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to reply to you. We keep support emails for 12 months after the conversation ends and then delete them. You can ask us to delete your emails sooner at any time; we will do so within 30 days and confirm by reply.

## Children

The tool is not aimed at children and does not collect information from anyone.
